Tue 2
How Medical Claims Work — End to End
Goal: Understand the full claim lifecycle, CPT and ICD-10 codes, and what a real claim form looks like. This is the domain knowledge your dashboard will be built on.
⏱ ~4 hours 📚 Domain Learning 🗂 Hands On
📄
Morning: The Claim Lifecycle & Coding Systems
CMS resources + guided reading on CPT and ICD-10
~2 hrs ▼
🔴
CMS MLN — Medicare Learning Network Products
Search "claim submission" and "denial" — CMS publishes plain-language guides on how claims are submitted and adjudicated. Read the "Medicare Claims Processing" fact sheet.
Free · CMS.gov
🩺
AMA — What Is a CPT Code?
The American Medical Association's plain-English explanation of CPT codes — the 5-digit codes that describe every medical procedure billed to insurance.
Free · AMA-ASSN.org
🏥
CDC — ICD-10-CM Official Information
ICD-10 diagnosis codes — the alphanumeric codes that describe why a patient was seen. Understanding how CPT (what was done) and ICD-10 (why) must "match" is critical for billing.
Free · CDC.gov
Reading tasks
📋
Afternoon: Look at a Real Claim Form
Get an anonymized CMS-1500 form from the practice — identify every field
~1.5 hrs ▼
📄
CMS-1500 Claim Form (Blank)
The standard paper claim form used for physician billing. Download and annotate it — even though claims are electronic today, this form contains every field that matters.
Free · CMS.gov PDF
🔒 HIPAA reminder: if using a real form, confirm with Dad that all 18 patient identifiers are removed or replaced with fictitious values before you work with it.

📋
CARC / RARC Code Lookup
Claim Adjustment Reason Codes — the official codes payers use to explain denials. Look up: CO-4, CO-16, CO-18, CO-22, CO-97, PR-1, PR-2. Know what each means.
Free · CARC.com
📈
HFMA — Revenue Cycle Denial Management Resources
The Healthcare Financial Management Association publishes practical denial management content. Skim their denial category overviews.
Free · HFMA.org

Morning: Write the Dashboard Requirements Document
This is the Week 1 deliverable — treat it like a professional memo
~2 hrs ▼
📋 Use this structure for the document: (1) Purpose — what problem does ClaimPulse solve? (2) Users — who will use it and when? (3) Metrics — the 7 KPIs with formulas and definitions (4) Data Source — what file/export will feed the dashboard? (5) Filters needed — date range, payer, provider? (6) Priority — which 3 metrics are most critical?
