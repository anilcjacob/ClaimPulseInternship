Build the ROI Model — Real Numbers from Real People
Quantify the business value of ClaimPulse. Interview billing staff, gather real numbers, and build a credible ROI model. Not guesses — actual time measurements and denial rate data.
⚡
Day 66 Tasks
▼
Daily Quest+25 bonus XP
The ROI model has assumptions. For each assumption, write a best-case, base-case, and worst-case scenario. Present the base case in the deck but be prepared to defend any number with its assumption. Interviewers and leadership will probe the weakest assumption — know which one it is.
✏️
End-of-Day Check-in
▼
"State the 3 most compelling numbers from the ROI model — the ones that would make a practice administrator sit up. For each: what is the number, how was it calculated, and what assumption are you least confident about?"

Day 66 done.

Build the 8-Slide Deck with Claude in PowerPoint
Use Claude in PowerPoint to draft the slides from the business case document. Refine the AI-generated content into a presentation that a non-technical leadership audience will find clear and compelling.
⚡
Day 67 Tasks
▼
Daily Quest+25 bonus XP
Ask Claude in PowerPoint to rewrite Slide 6 (Business Impact) three ways: (a) optimistic, (b) conservative, (c) 'what could go wrong' — compare the three. Which version would you present to a skeptical CFO vs a supportive billing manager? The audience changes the framing.
✏️
End-of-Day Check-in
▼
"Which slide will be hardest to deliver convincingly — not to build, but to present under scrutiny? Why? What is the one piece of evidence that would make that slide airtight?"

Day 67 done.

Wed68
Demo Choreography — Practice Both Live Demos
Plan and practice the live demo sequence for both interfaces. Every click must be deliberate. A fumbled demo undermines the entire presentation regardless of the quality of the build.
⚡
Day 68 Tasks
▼
Daily Quest+25 bonus XP
Ask Claude Desktop: 'What is our denial rate this month, which payer has the highest denial rate, and what are our top 3 denied CPT codes?' — this is a single message that calls all 3 tools. Use this as the climax of Demo 2. Time how long it takes to get the complete answer. Compare this to how long the same analysis would take manually.
✏️
End-of-Day Check-in
▼
"What is the one thing that could go wrong in the live demo, and what is your plan B if it does? Preparation is not about expecting failure — it's about removing the anxiety that comes from not having a plan."

Day 68 done.

Thu69
Presentation Slides Final + Handout One-Pager
Final polish on all 8 slides. Write a half-page handout for the audience summarizing what ClaimPulse does and the key business metrics — something tangible for people to take away.
⚡
Day 69 Tasks
▼
Daily Quest+25 bonus XP
Show slide 6 (business impact) to someone outside the practice — a family member or friend. Do they understand the ROI argument without context? If they need explanation, the slide is not finished. A great slide communicates its own argument.
✏️
End-of-Day Check-in
▼
"After rehearsing the full 20-minute presentation — which section do you feel most confident about, and which feels weakest? The weak section is where to spend Thursday evening."

Day 69 done.

Fri70
Week 4 Review + Final Preparation Checklist
The deck is built. The demos are practiced. Write the final preparation checklist for Fall Week 5's rehearsal sessions and confirm all technical systems are in order.
⚡
Day 70 Tasks
▼
Daily Quest+25 bonus XP
Ask your tutor to role-play as a skeptical CFO: 'Challenge me on the ROI numbers as if you're a CFO who doesn't believe AI tools save as much time as claimed.' Practice defending each assumption. The best presentations survive scrutiny — they're built on evidence, not optimism.
✏️
End-of-Day Check-in
▼
"In 3 sentences or fewer: what is ClaimPulse, what problem does it solve, and what does the data say about its value? If you can't say this without hesitation, practice it until you can — this is the answer to the question every leadership presentation ends with: 'So what?'"

Day 70 done.
